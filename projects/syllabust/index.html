Syllabust

in development. a closed beta is coming soon.

Syllabust turns a course syllabus into a semester plan. Start with a PDF, a DOCX, or text you paste in.

It leads with study blocks. The plan sets aside time to study around your classes, then exports those blocks as an .ics file you can add to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.

It also brings the rest of the syllabus into the same plan: due dates, readings, class meetings, and office hours. Office hours sit on the calendar with everything else, ready for a real conversation with the person teaching the course.

Harrison is building Syllabust for students. He is a higher-ed professional, and he wanted a syllabus to become a plan you can live with for the whole term.
