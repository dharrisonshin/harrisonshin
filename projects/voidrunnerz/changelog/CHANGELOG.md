# voidrunnerz changelog

Newest first. The desk keeps a short What's new list for the current build. This file is every build, in the order the game grew.

## Build 14

Nov 24, 2026

- Wave labels match the lane, and the pay notes match the contract. A briefing names a kamikaze rush, a sniper ambush, or a carrier escort only when that enemy is on the job.
- Split Lane fragile cargo is 6% of the contract pay per hit, and the cut stops at 75%. Glass Run is 10% per hit, and the cut stops at 60%.
- More desk scenes between runs. After later boss kills, Ma worries about Nova, warns that other outfits will come calling, and Dex teases off-book pay. Nova has a bounty story she was not allowed to fly. A few short lines show up on quiet returns. Each one plays once. Esc skips. Nothing talks during a flight.
- The first boss is still Nova putting the drinks on you.
- The tip button opens the right page.

## Build 13

Oct 31, 2026

- Sound resumes when you come back to the tab. Leaving and returning no longer leaves the game silent.

## Build 12

Oct 31, 2026

- Desk scenes between runs line up instead of cutting each other off. You can get more than one talk after a run, and you can still skip.
- Ma points at every new red wanted poster.
- The first time you drop a boss, Nova's drinks are on you.

## Build 11

Oct 31, 2026

- Ma speaks when you first pick Old Iron. Nova speaks when you first pick Daybreak.
- Those ship scenes, and Nova's veteran scene, play once.
- Each Yard purchase makes the next lanes a little busier.
- Shop cards picked up clearer icons, and the hull block on the desk is labeled Ship.

## Build 10

Oct 30, 2026

- The game has sound: shots, hits, and the desk. M mutes. The volume slider is remembered. Phones start muted.

## Build 9

Oct 30, 2026

- Quiet Haul, Split Lane, and Glass Run ease off for early runs. New threats wait until you have flown a bit.
- The desk has side rails for a tip, dispatches, and how to play.
- P or Esc pauses a flight. Esc still skips dialogue first.
- The cone beam, if you own one, is the Down arrow on a desktop, and the beam button on a phone.

## Build 8

Oct 30, 2026

- New enemies: cloakers, snipers, cargo thieves, and the rest of the Threat Log. Entries fill in the first time you meet them.
- Every run plays different. A contract draws from its own pool, then may add a wave, a twist, or a surprise.
- Black Contracts live in the Yard shop after your first boss. They pay 1,600 if you dock clean. A patrol tag is an arrest and a 400 credit fine.
- Bounty contracts show up on the board. High risk, one boss, 900 credits.
- The ships have their names: Second Chance, Old Iron, and Daybreak. Old Iron and Daybreak unlock from run 2.

## Build 7

Oct 30, 2026

- On a wide screen the lane is 640 across, with room to slip shots. Phones stay on the tall 390 lane.
- The desk tells you how to launch, how fragile cargo works, and that the browser keeps your credits and your gear.

## Build 6

Oct 30, 2026

- FIRE and the bomb stack sit above the corner, clear of the home indicator, so a thumb can reach them.

## Build 5

Oct 30, 2026

- The front page is an enlistment poster for voidrunnerz. Ma, Oddie, Nova, and Dex are on it.
- Enlist & Run opens the game.
- On a desktop, W A S D moves and Space shoots. Arrow keys use a bomb or a beam you are carrying. The mouse does not fly the ship.

## Build 4

Oct 30, 2026

- Ma Kessler, Oddie, Dex Varga, and Nova Reyes have their portraits on the desk, in the shop, and on the pay slip.

## Build 3

Oct 30, 2026

- Dex sometimes offers an off-book crate. It pays 1,600 if you are not caught.
- A patrol tag pays nothing for the crate, shows an arrest, and takes a 400 credit fine. The fine stops at zero.

## Build 2

Oct 30, 2026

- Three hulls: Courier, Heavy Hauler, and Interceptor. They differ in speed, fire, and how many hits they can take. The browser remembers the one you pick.
- Some contracts carry fragile cargo. Each hull hit cuts the contract pay, down to a floor. The card states the rate. The pay slip shows the cut. Combat pay and leftover hull are not cut.

## Build 1

Oct 30, 2026

- voidrunnerz is a delivery run. Pick a contract, fly the lane, shoot raiders and weavers, and dodge debris.
- Warzone Cut adds laser sweeps. Slip them by moving up or down.
- Dock the crate and the pay goes into a credit bank saved in this browser. A wreck pays nothing for that crate.
