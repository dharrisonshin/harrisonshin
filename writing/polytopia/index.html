Why The Battle of Polytopia Is the Best Strategy Game You’re Not Playing

nov 10, 2026

games

I grew up on strategy games. I’ve played StarCraft, StarCraft II, Warcraft III, Civilization VI, Age of Empires, and Age of Mythology. I first heard about The Battle of Polytopia in Walter Isaacson’s biography of Elon Musk. I downloaded it out of curiosity, and I’ve been hooked ever since. It’s a little low-poly game about tiny tribes fighting over a square world, and I think everyone should play it.

A real 4X game that fits in your pocket

Polytopia has everything that made Civ VI eat my weekends: you explore, expand, research technologies, and go to war. It just strips out the busywork. A full game takes minutes instead of a weekend. The developer says most mobile players play it in portrait mode, with one thumb. That isn’t a watered-down strategy game. It’s a well-designed one.

Every game is different

Maps are generated fresh every time, and you can pick from six map types: Dryland, Lake, Pangea, Continents, Archipelago, and Water World. There are 16 tribes, each with its own strengths and personality. Some games you’re a sea power island-hopping on rafts. Other times you’re rushing a neighbor before turn 10. Weekly challenges and tournaments keep it fresh even after years.

It respects your wallet

On mobile, it’s free with four tribes, and you can play them as much as you want. The other tribes and skins are one-time purchases. There’s no in-game currency and no pay-to-win. Midjiwan’s CEO has called aggressive monetization “truly bizarre.” He wants every purchase to feel joyful, not guilty. On PC and Switch, you pay once and get everything.

I’m still not a master, and that’s the fun

I’ve played all those strategy classics, and I’m by no means a master strategist. Polytopia reminds me of that every few games. You can learn the basics in one game, but reading the map, managing your stars, and timing an attack take way longer. Every loss teaches me something. Every win makes me want to try a new tribe or a harder opponent. I keep learning how to be a little better, and that’s what keeps me coming back.

You can play it with anyone

It has online multiplayer for up to 16 players, cross-platform matchmaking, leaderboards, and pass-and-play.

Don’t take my word for it

It’s been around for almost a decade and has more than 20 million downloads on mobile. On Steam, it’s rated Overwhelmingly Positive, with 95% of reviews positive. It runs on iPhone, Android, Steam, Switch, and even Tesla cars.

Play with me

If you want a game, add me on iOS. My friend code is FYGDU-SERKQ. Send me an invite, and I’ll take my turns whenever I’ve got a minute. Fair warning: I’m still learning, so you might win.

If you’ve ever bounced off a strategy game because it felt like homework, give Polytopia one game. It’s free on your phone, and you’ll be “one more turn”-ing at 1 AM before you know it.
